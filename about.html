Predictive intelligence for infrastructure decisions before consequences become obvious.

Praedicta Inc. is a U.S. GeoAI company developing predictive mapping and decision-intelligence systems for infrastructure, mobility, resilience, public safety, and complex spatial systems.

The company transforms geospatial and institutional data into evidence about structural pressure, emerging risk, network dependencies, and potential consequences — helping decision-makers understand conditions before critical choices are locked in.

Founder

Anuar Aubakirov — Founder

Praedicta was founded by Anuar Aubakirov, a civic technologist and GeoAI developer focused on predictive mapping, infrastructure intelligence, and decision-support systems.

His work has included road-safety analytics, predictive mobility systems, infrastructure-risk mapping, flood and evacuation modeling, and geospatial decision tools across U.S. and international locations.
Mission

Clarity under uncertainty.

Praedicta exists to help institutions see structural pressure, emerging risk, and potential consequences more clearly before important decisions are locked in.

The company aims to build predictive intelligence that is technically rigorous, transparent about uncertainty, portable across jurisdictions, and useful to the people responsible for infrastructure and operational decisions.
