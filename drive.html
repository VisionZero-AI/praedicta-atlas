Research Preview. Praedicta Drive displays structural road context from the Praedicta Pittsburgh Road Context API. Structural percentiles are available only for road segments within the retrospective validation cohort. A null percentile means validation information is unavailable; it does not mean zero risk.

This interface does not provide real-time collision warnings, calibrated crash probabilities, safety certification, or safe/unsafe road classifications. Do not interact with this interface while operating a vehicle.
