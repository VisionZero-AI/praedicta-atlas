Praedicta AI Governance Charter

Institutional Integrity • Sovereign Respect • Planning-First Intelligence

Praedicta AI operates as a governance-aligned civic intelligence platform. We design structural risk models that respect sovereign data policies, institutional authority, and public-sector accountability.

1. Respect for Sovereign Data Architecture

We operate strictly within the legal and institutional data frameworks of each jurisdiction. Where crash-level or incident-level datasets are not publicly released, we do not attempt to bypass or challenge such policy.

Our models are built exclusively on legally available structural, geospatial, infrastructure, and planning datasets.

2. No Use of Sensitive Microdata

Praedicta AI does not ingest or collect:

  • Personally identifiable information (PII)
  • Police crash reports
  • Enforcement-level case records
  • Individual behavioral tracking data
  • Surveillance feeds

Structural intelligence is developed without dependency on individual-level data.

3. Derived Risk, Not Incident Exposure

Our outputs represent derived structural risk indicators based on geometry, density, land use, infrastructure configuration, and network topology.

We do not publish individual crash points for stigmatization or neighborhood labeling.

4. Planning-First, Enforcement-Neutral

Praedicta AI supports long-term planning, infrastructure prioritization, resilience modeling, and master plan evaluation.

Our systems are not surveillance tools and are not built for enforcement operations.

5. Advisory-Layer Architecture

All outputs are advisory and analytical in nature. Praedicta AI does not replace professional engineering judgment, regulatory authority, or statutory processes.

6. Institutional Neutrality

We do not rank governments, criticize data transparency frameworks, or use predictive analytics as policy leverage.

Praedicta AI maintains institutional neutrality across jurisdictions.

7. Data Ownership & Intellectual Property

Governments and public agencies retain ownership of their datasets. Praedicta AI monetizes analytical methodology and derived intelligence, not raw public data.

8. Privacy-by-Design Engineering

Our architecture follows data-minimization principles. We intentionally avoid ingestion of personal identifiers and design models around structural signals.

9. Governance Compatibility

Praedicta AI is designed to align with common public-sector governance frameworks, including:

  • Data minimization principles
  • Open-data compliance standards
  • Planning transparency requirements
  • Risk advisory best practices

Core Ethical Commitment

Public safety innovation must respect institutional sovereignty.

If crash data is not public, we innovate without it.
