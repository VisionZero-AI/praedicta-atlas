Pilot Method

From decision question to deployable intelligence

1. Define Establish the decision question, geography, users, constraints, and evidence requirements.
2. Integrate Assemble relevant public, authoritative, institutional, or client-provided datasets.
3. Model Apply geospatial modeling, network analysis, machine learning, and consequence-analysis methods.
4. Deliver Produce maps, rankings, scenarios, evidence layers, documentation, and decision-ready outputs.
5. Deploy Expand successful pilots into recurring models, operational workflows, data products, or APIs.
Engagement Paths

Start focused. Expand when the evidence supports it.

Typical Deliverables

Decision-ready outputs, not just visualization

  • Interactive predictive or consequence-intelligence map
  • Documented methodology and data provenance
  • Priority locations, corridors, assets, or risk segments

  • GeoJSON, CSV, analytical tables, or other agreed data outputs
  • Decision brief summarizing findings, limitations, and next steps
  • Recommendations for operational deployment or continued modeling
